Each amber box is one queued card. Select a card, then either click its corners TL → TR → BR → BL (top-left of the printed card first, so a rotated card starts at its printed top-left) or press B to seed the box and drag the handles. Corners hidden by another card: press O on that corner. Corners beyond the photo edge are marked automatically. S skips a target you cannot label. Labels are saved to a separate journal; nothing here changes the dataset.
