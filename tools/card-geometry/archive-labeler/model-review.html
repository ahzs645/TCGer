What to look for

Does every real card have one outline? Do the four points touch the physical corners? Is the crop upright, without another card or background cutting into it? For overlaps, keep the full card outline and mark it partly covered. Preview crops show the photographed pixels, including any covering card. Reference masks may describe only the visible part.
