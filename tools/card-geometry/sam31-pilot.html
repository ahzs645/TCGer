How to read this trial

Two fixed text prompts were tested at a 0.50 confidence threshold on 12 selected photos with 51 saved card appearances. They include difficult failures and varied library examples, so this is not a representative benchmark or a model promotion test. Agreement counts require a one-to-one match at polygon/mask IoU ≥0.50; on occluded cards this is not visible-mask accuracy. Unmatched masks may be real background cards. Drafts are shown only where a contour naturally simplifies to a convex quadrilateral; printed orientation stays unknown.
