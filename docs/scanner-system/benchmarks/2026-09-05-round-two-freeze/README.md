# Round-two corpus and experiment freeze — 2026-09-05

The repaired trainers and train-split self-evaluation were completed first, followed by the [incumbent reruns on the v6-full successor](../2026-09-04-incumbents-v6-successor/). This directory records the subsequent corpus/configuration freeze, before any round-two training result.

The release `card-geometry-training-round-two-v1` contains 15,744 images and 45,172 card instances: 13,465 training images / 39,934 instances and 2,279 validation images / 5,238 instances. It combines 5,744 real archive images with 10,000 newly rendered synthetic scenes. Evaluation records remain in separately pinned releases. The training corpus hash is `286d1196e9f0c85a37779ec52c6e9ba2f1533224a62c066dc93269c26720dbc4`.

Synthetic scenes use 3,400 split-separated card faces plus three training-only backs and [60 reviewed external CC0 surface textures](../2026-09-05-cc0-backgrounds/). The backgrounds are not self-captured scene photographs; they do not add real hands, sleeves or binder scenes. The original 100 Dev Mode crops remain excluded. Every background retains its provider identity, review and file hash, and the exclusion inventory includes all 43 Dev Mode/evaluation sessions. The combined release carries the source manifests and metadata under `provenance/`; preflight verifies those bytes against the assembly inventory covered by the corpus hash.

The frozen [training-minimums-v3 policy](../../../../tools/card-geometry/policies/training-minimums-v3.json) has SHA-256 `679dd02c8e6280f2043978e007ea16d9608eba9a0c74ea2766477b885c4e56da`. Its training/validation minima apply to this mixed corpus, with external evaluation releases pinned separately. Archive and fork mappings, source asset IDs, physical card IDs, sessions and exact image hashes must be disjoint across the applicable boundaries. The near-duplicate audit uses DCT pHash at Hamming distance <= 4 with rotations/reflections; it is a diagnostic in addition to the exact gates.

All four candidate configurations use 640-pixel input, 50 epochs, one repeat, seed 20260905, and no runtime augmentation. Real images receive black context padding of `ceil(0.15 * max(width, height))` on each side; this policy is required by experiment schema v2 and covered by the shared fairness hash. Synthetic records retain their declared 192-pixel margin on a 1280-pixel long side. Candidate-specific optimizer/framework settings are pinned by the tooling revision and recorded by each trainer. The YOLOX validation fix and batch-scaled learning rate remain enabled.

Ingestion checks retain all 45,172 instance labels, including 5,856 unknown-corner boxes with YOLO keypoint visibility zero. FastViT targets are finite for every record and ignore negative heatmap loss over unknown boxes; no records were skipped for missing boxes. Decoded image tensors were additionally checked for one image per canonical archive/split. Known corners from polygon fits remain training supervision; the readiness policy continues to exclude mask-fit corners from metric-eligible truth.

The evaluation keys are `frozenReal` and `syntheticMultigame`. They pin the alias-compatible v6-full and multigame successors, whose record/image bytes, splits and slices are unchanged from their predecessors. Benchmark reports retain both successor and predecessor corpus hashes. Each job saves completed training output before automatically running those geometry benchmarks and recognition replay. YOLO11n/s remain evaluation-only candidates.

Full preflight passed all 19 checks with `readyFor: training`. Real archive source identity is accepted at record level (`grouping.sourceAssetIds`) or on every instance; synthetic scenes still require each card asset identity. Regression fixtures cover both valid real representations, missing real identity, and a synthetic record that lacks per-instance identities.

Machine-readable evidence: `corpus-summary.json`, `synthetic-build-summary.json`, `cross-release-leakage.json`, `ingestion-check.json`, `preflight-report.json`, `near-duplicates/audit.json`, `dataset-publication.json`, `configs/`, and `freeze.json`. Publication and launch receipts are recorded separately so this frozen input record does not imply that training has completed. Tooling validation: 217 geometry tests and 14 compositor tests passed; Ruff 0.15.8 passed.

The perceptual audit flagged 14 pairs (seven across training/validation, seven against synthetic evaluation). All were visually reviewed and retained as distinct scenes: 13 share a low-frequency procedural playmat/ring pattern but have different cards, layouts and perspectives; the remaining pair has different surface textures and card art. No flagged pair involved real evaluation. `near-duplicates/review-decisions.json` records each decision with both image hashes, reviewer and review-sheet reference. This is a documented synthetic template similarity, and the pHash audit is not a proof that all possible near-duplicates are absent.
